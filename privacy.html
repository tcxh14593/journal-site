Privacy policy

Journal · Last updated 11 October 2026

In short: your journal stays with you. The developer never receives, sees or stores any of it.

What Journal stores on your device

Your pages, typed text, handwriting, photos you add, stickers you import and your settings are saved inside the app on your phone or PC. If you set a PIN, only a scrambled (hashed) version of it is kept, never the PIN itself. Fingerprint unlock is handled entirely by your phone’s operating system; Journal never sees or stores your fingerprint.

Photos and camera

Journal only uses photos you choose from your gallery or take with the camera inside the app. It does not look through your other photos.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync is off unless you turn it on. If you choose “Phone + Google Drive” and pick a Google account, Journal:

  • creates a folder called “Journal” in that account’s Google Drive and saves copies of your pages and their photos there, so the same journal can open on your other device;
  • uses Google’s drive.file permission, which only lets it see and change files that Journal itself created. It cannot see any of your other Drive files;
  • reads your Google account’s email address only to show which account is connected.

Your synced journal is stored in your own Google Drive and is covered by Google’s privacy policy. It is not sent anywhere else.

What Journal does not do

No ads. No analytics or tracking. No selling or sharing of data. No servers run by the developer: data only moves between your device and your own Google Drive.

Google API Services

Journal’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Delete pages inside the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling Journal removes everything it saved on that device.
  • To remove the synced copy, delete the “Journal” folder in your Google Drive.
  • To stop Journal’s access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Journal.
