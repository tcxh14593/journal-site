Nine Lives

Nine Lives is a free-form, page-by-page journal app for Android and Windows. Write with a stylus, finger or keyboard, add photos, stickers, washi tape and frames, and arrange everything freely in layers.

Your pages are saved on your own phone. If you choose, Nine Lives can also keep a copy in your own Google Drive so the same journal opens on your Windows PC.

Nine Lives is a small personal project. It has no ads, no accounts of its own and no servers.
